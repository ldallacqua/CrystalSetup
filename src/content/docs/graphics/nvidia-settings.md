## NVIDIA Control Panel Settings

Optimizing the driver-level settings for the best balance of quality and performance.

### Manage 3D Settings (Program Settings: ffxiv_dx11.exe)

- **Image Sharpening**: On, set to **50% or less**. FFXIV has a natively soft image presentation; bumping the sharpness in the control panel accounts for this softness without introducing artifacts.
- **Low Latency Mode**: On / Ultra.
- **Max Frame Rate**: Match your monitor's refresh rate or slightly below for G-SYNC.
- **Power Management Mode**: Prefer maximum performance.
- **Texture Filtering - Quality**: High Quality.
- **Vertical Sync**: Off (if using G-SYNC + In-game limiter/Reflex).

### Digital Vibrance
- Suggested: +5% to +10% for slightly more pop in Eorzea.
